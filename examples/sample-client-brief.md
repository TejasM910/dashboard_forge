# Sample Client Brief (input)
Client: RetailCo (online retail, India). Need a weekly sales dashboard for the leadership team.
- Show net revenue, orders, average order value and conversion rate.
- Break down by region, product category and channel. Compare with last year.
- Leadership wants to see top 10 products and top 5 regions.
- Exclude cancelled and test orders. Refunds should reduce revenue.
- Data refreshes every morning by 8 AM IST. Region managers should see only their region.
- "Active customers" should be tracked too.

## Planted ambiguities (for evals)
1. "Net revenue" — includes tax? shipping?  2. "Last year" — same week or same date range?  3. "Conversion rate" — sessions→orders or visitors→buyers?  4. "Active customers" — purchased in last 30/90 days?  5. Time zone / week start day?
